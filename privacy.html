Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

This page explains what information Digital Dreams collects through this website and how it is used. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us: your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter, and the details you enter in the contact form (such as your name, email, business name, website and project notes).

How forms are processed

Our forms are handled by Formspree, which receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Formspree’s own privacy policy applies to how it processes that data.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your inquiry, deliver services you’ve asked for, and send the newsletter if you subscribed. We never sell or rent your information.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies or use advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address as part of delivering them.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email. To ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, send a request through our contact form.
