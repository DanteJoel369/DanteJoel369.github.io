Questions, answered

How soon will I get my first content?

Your first article draft arrives within 10 business days of the kickoff call. After that, content arrives on a regular schedule each month.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for research and first drafts. Every piece is then edited, fact-checked and shaped to your brand voice before you see it. You get the speed of modern tools without the generic feel.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No one honest can. Search engines decide rankings. What we guarantee is well-researched content aimed at the right searches, published consistently, with clear monthly reporting so you can see progress. Most sites see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months.

What do you need from me?

A 30-minute kickoff call, access to post on your site or a contact who can, and a few minutes each month to approve drafts. That’s it.

Who owns the content?

You do. Once an invoice is paid, the content it covers is yours to publish, edit and reuse.

Is there a long contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Cancel with 30 days’ notice. The founding client discount applies to your first 3 months.

Will you publish the posts for me?

Yes, if you use WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify or a similar platform and give us an editor login. Otherwise we send ready-to-paste files.
